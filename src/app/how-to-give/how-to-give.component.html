How to Give

What To Do...

If you are interested in donating to Mizzou Racing,

  1. Gifts intended for Mizzou Racing must be made to the College of Engineering page here
  2. Under "Designation" make sure that "Engineering Dean's Fund for Excellence" is listed
  3. Fill in all other information on the form and check out when you are ready
  4. Email Mizzou Racing at sae@missouri.edu, Ashley Simmons at simmonsar@missouri.edu, or Melissa Amos-landgraf at mamos-landgraf@missouri.edu with the following information:*
    1. Your Name
    2. Total Gift Amount
    3. Amount of gift to designate for Mizzou Racing

Alternatively, checks can be made out as payable to "The University of Missouri" with the memo line "Engineering Dean's Fund for Excellence". Then, a check stub or separate note must also be included which designates the amount to Mizzou Racing or other College of Engineering entities as you wish.


*This step is necessary to ensure the funds reach Mizzou Racing
