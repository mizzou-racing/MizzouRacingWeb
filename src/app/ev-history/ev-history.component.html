Our History

Mizzou Electric Racing, previously known as Mizzou Eco Racing, has been a university organization since 1993. During its tenure, the Eco Racing team was involved in building solar cars, hydrogen cars, and an electric conversion of a Chevy S10.

Mizzou Eco Racing's Solar Car Team competed annually in the American Solar Challenge, also known as “Sunrayce,” where cars raced from Chicago to Los Angeles over a 10-day period. Mizzou Eco Racing transitioned to building hydrogen cars in 2011, competing in the Shell Eco-Marathon from 2011 to 2018. In 2017, Mizzou Eco Racing's Electric Car Team successfully converted a Chevy S10 into an electric vehicle.

In 2022, Mizzou Eco Racing became Mizzou Electric Racing. Together with Mizzou Racing, we moved from the basement of Naka Hall into a new shop in Lafferre Hall. In the same year, we began the joint manufacturing of Mizzou’s very first Formula SAE Electric Vehicle.
